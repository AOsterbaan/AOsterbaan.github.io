Directions

For a photo-induced process to proceed, light must be absorbed. Consequently, the light intensity in photosensitive media decreases with depth. This interactive graph demonstrates the relationship between key variables. Use the sliders to adjust Gaussian parameters and observe how they integrate with imported absorption data.
